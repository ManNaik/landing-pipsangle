# Review rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5. Publishing needs accuracy 5, compliance 5, and at least 4 everywhere else.

## Accuracy

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 5 | Every claim checked against a live source and verified, including implied causes and comparisons. Numbers, units, periods and dates exact. |
| 4 | Facts are right, but one wording overstates or blurs what a source says, or implies a cause no source gives. |
| 3 | One factual error or one claim with no source. |
| 1-2 | Several errors, or made-up figures or quotes. |

## Originality

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 5 | Adds real explanation or connection the sources don't make. Clearly written from scratch. |
| 4 | Original writing with some added context. Doesn't mirror any source's structure. |
| 3 | Mostly a re-wording of one or two sources. |
| 1-2 | Close paraphrase or copied passages. |

## Helpfulness

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 5 | Answers the searcher's questions directly and leaves the reader knowing what the event means for their trading, without telling them to trade. |
| 4 | Useful and clear, with a minor gap. |
| 3 | Accurate but generic. A reader learns little beyond the headline. |
| 1-2 | Misses the point of the search, or is padding. |

## Engagement

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 5 | Strong, specific opening. Easy to scan: short paragraphs, descriptive headings, concrete examples. Reads like a person wrote it. |
| 4 | Flows well, with a couple of flat spots. |
| 3 | Dry or repetitive, with long paragraphs or stock phrasing. |
| 1-2 | Hard to read, or reads like generic AI copy. |

## SEO

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 5 | Title and excerpt are specific and match the search intent. Keyword used naturally early on. Headings mirror real questions. Site and source links are relevant. |
| 4 | Solid basics with one missed opportunity. |
| 3 | Keyword forced or missing from key places, or a vague title. |
| 1-2 | Keyword stuffing, a misleading title, or the wrong intent. |

## Compliance

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 5 | Informational only. No predictions stated as fact, no advice, no hype. Balanced about risk. Accurate about PipsAngel. Quotes short and attributed. |
| 4 | One borderline phrase that leans toward a call or overstates certainty. |
| 1-3 | Advice, guarantees, misleading claims, or unattributed copying. |
