# Where to look

## Official sources (use for numbers, dates and decisions)

| Area | Source |
|------|--------|
| US jobs, CPI, PPI | Bureau of Labor Statistics (bls.gov): release schedule and news releases |
| US GDP, PCE inflation | Bureau of Economic Analysis (bea.gov) |
| US retail sales | Census Bureau (census.gov) |
| Fed decisions, statements, minutes, projections | Federal Reserve (federalreserve.gov): FOMC calendar and press releases |
| Euro area rates and statements | European Central Bank (ecb.europa.eu) |
| Euro area inflation, GDP | Eurostat (ec.europa.eu/eurostat) |
| UK rates | Bank of England (bankofengland.co.uk) |
| UK inflation, jobs, GDP | Office for National Statistics (ons.gov.uk) |
| Japan rates, yen intervention | Bank of Japan (boj.or.jp), Ministry of Finance Japan |
| Australia | Reserve Bank of Australia (rba.gov.au), ABS (abs.gov.au) |
| Canada | Bank of Canada (bankofcanada.ca), Statistics Canada (statcan.gc.ca) |
| Switzerland | Swiss National Bank (snb.ch) |
| New Zealand | RBNZ (rbnz.govt.nz), Stats NZ (stats.govt.nz) |
| India | Reserve Bank of India (rbi.org.in) |
| Rate expectations | CME FedWatch (cmegroup.com) |
| Oil | U.S. EIA (eia.gov), OPEC (opec.org) |
| PMIs | S&P Global (spglobal.com), ISM (ismworld.org) |
| Positioning | CFTC Commitments of Traders (cftc.gov) |
| Global context | IMF, BIS, OECD |

The benchmark recognises these publishers as official. At least one must back every article.

## Demand signals

- **Economic calendars** (Forex Factory, Investing.com, the Moneycontrol calendar, broker calendars): impact ratings and timing. Read them for context; don't copy their tables.
- **News coverage**: how many major outlets covered the topic in the last 24-48 hours (Reuters, Bloomberg, CNBC, FT, WSJ, FXStreet, DailyFX, Investing.com).
- **Trader questions**: "People also ask" style questions in search results, Reddit r/Forex, Forex Factory forums, Babypips forums. Note thread engagement, such as comments and recency.
- **Search results**: what ranks for the keyword, how recent it is, and whether it answers the question directly.

## Topic types that work

- **Event preview** (1-7 days before): what's expected, why it matters, what different outcomes have meant for currencies in the past.
- **Event recap** (0-2 days after): what happened against forecasts and how the market reacted, with sources.
- **Explainer tied to the news**: a concept people search for that today's event makes relevant.
